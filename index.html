How Does The Speaker Cleaner Sound Work?

If your phone dropped into the sink, pool, or got caught in heavy rain, water droplets often become trapped inside the micro-perforations of the speaker mesh. This creates a surface tension barrier, preventing sound waves from traveling out freely and causing audio to sound muffled, scratchy, or barely audible.

Fix My Speakers utilizes the Web Audio API to generate specific low-frequency sine waves (concentrated around 165Hz). When played at maximum volume, this sound wave forces the internal speaker membrane (the diaphragm) to oscillate with maximum amplitude. This rapid mechanical movement displaces air inside the acoustic chamber, building pressure that expels trapped water and particles out of the speaker enclosure.

Step-by-Step Instructions to Clear Muffled Speakers

  1. Disconnect Bluetooth Devices: Ensure headphones or wireless speakers are disconnected so the audio plays strictly from your phone's internal speakers.
  2. Crank Volume to Max: Increase media volume to 100% to maximize kinetic acoustic force.
  3. Angle the Device: Hold your phone upright or facing downwards, allowing gravity to assist in dislodging liquid droplets.
  4. Run the Ejector Cycle: Tap the button above and let the sound play for at least 60 seconds. Repeat 2–3 times if droplets continue to appear.
  5. Dab with a Towel: Gently absorb emerging moisture using a lint-free cloth or tissue.

Water Eject vs. Dust Sweeping: Which Should You Use?

Water Ejection Mode: Uses a heavy, low-frequency pulse (165Hz). Water requires low-frequency air displacement because high frequencies create small, fast vibrations that cannot push liquid mass effectively.

Dust Sweeper Mode: Uses a sweep ranging from 200Hz to 1200Hz. Dust particles settle within dry crevices; alternating the pitch breaks particle friction and shakes out accumulated lint and debris.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this identical to the Apple Watch Water Lock feature?

Yes. The Apple Watch Series 2 and newer use a dedicated speaker vibration sound to eject water after swimming. Our web utility generates the same physical pressure dynamics across any smartphone or tablet.

Should I place my phone in rice after speaker cleaning?

No. Placing electronics in raw rice is a proven myth. Rice dust can enter your charging port and speaker grill, mixing with moisture to form a sticky paste. Instead, use acoustic ejection and let the device air dry in an open room with good airflow.

Does this work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Because the audio is produced directly inside your modern mobile browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge) without requiring third-party app installations, it is 100% compatible with iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows.
