About Us & The Science of Acoustic Cleaning

Our Mission

FixMySpeakers Pro was built to provide an instant, zero-cost, and non-invasive hardware remedy for smartphone users facing accidental water ingress or dust accumulation.

The Physics of Water Ejection

Liquid behaves uniquely inside micro-enclosures like smartphone speaker grills. Due to capillary action and high surface tension, small droplets stick to the mesh and cannot drain spontaneously.

Rather than using hazardous techniques—such as inserting needles, blowing compressed air, or heating devices with hair dryers—our web application utilizes the Web Audio API to generate targeted 165Hz sine waves. This specific frequency matches the physical resonance needed to maximize diaphragm excursion, transforming the speaker cone into a miniature air piston that mechanically expels moisture safely.

Zero Installations, Pure Local Processing

All sound synthesis occurs natively inside your browser. We never transmit your device telemetry to external servers, ensuring our utility is lightweight, instantaneous, and accessible worldwide even on low-bandwidth connections.
