Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Reviewed: October 2026

Commitment to User Privacy

At FixMySpeakers Pro, we believe basic hardware maintenance utilities should never require sacrificing personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details our technical architecture, explaining what minimal data is processed, why it is processed, and how your privacy rights are safeguarded under global privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Client-Side Audio Architecture (Zero Data Access)

The core functionality of FixMySpeakers Pro relies entirely on client-side software execution within your local web browser engine.

Local Audio Synthesis (W3C Web Audio API)

When you activate the Water Eject or Dust Sweeper features, all acoustic waveforms are computed dynamically within your browser via the native JavaScript AudioContext interface. No audio files are downloaded from external servers, and no acoustic data is recorded, saved, or uploaded.

Absence of Hardware Sensor Access

FixMySpeakers Pro never requests, accesses, or processes data from your device’s microphone, camera, accelerometer, contacts, local storage files, or biometric hardware. The tool operates solely as an audio output generator through standard media channels.

Data Collection and Usage Practices

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII). Any technical data processed falls strictly under the following categories:

Automated Server Access Logs

Like virtually all web properties, our hosting infrastructure automatically logs non-identifying technical metadata when your browser requests page assets. This includes:

  • Truncated or anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser engine and operating system versions (User-Agent string)
  • Referring webpage URLs
  • Date and timestamp of asset requests

This information is utilized solely to mitigate Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) threats, identify broken links, and optimize server response times. These logs are purged on an automated 30-day rolling cycle.

Aggregate Site Performance Analytics

We may employ privacy-first analytics tools (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or anonymized Google Analytics) that measure general site health, such as page load speed and high-level geographic regions. IP anonymization is permanently enabled, and personal profiling is prohibited.

Cookies and Local Storage Technologies

Essential Session Storage

We do not use invasive third-party cross-site advertising cookies. If you modify settings—such as toggling between dark/light themes or selecting a default frequency preset—we may retain this small configuration parameter within your browser’s localStorage. This state remains isolated on your device and is never shared with external tracking networks.

Third-Party Script Security

Any third-party Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) serving font files or CSS assets operate under strict zero-tracking configurations. No behavioral telemetry is collected via embedded assets.

Global Legal Frameworks & User Rights

European Economic Area (GDPR) Compliance

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, our lawful basis for handling technical server logs is Legitimate Interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) to maintain site security and deliver functional content. Because we do not store personal profiles, email addresses, or user identifiers, we operate under a strict data-minimization architecture. You retain the right to lodge inquiries regarding site security practices with your relevant Data Protection Authority.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell, rent, disclose, or trade personal consumer data to third parties. We do not participate in cross-context behavioral advertising. As a result, FixMySpeakers Pro has no requirement for an opt-out mechanism for the sale of personal information, as zero monetization of consumer data occurs.

Policy Modifications & Administrative Contact

We reserve the right to periodically update this documentation to reflect architectural changes or legislative amendments. Any revisions will be indicated by the updated "Last Reviewed" date at the top of this document.
