Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Do Not Collect

FixMySpeakers Pro operates completely client-side. The audio frequencies generated are created entirely within your browser's audio processor via the Web Audio API. We do not access, store, or transmit your microphone, camera, or personal device files.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not store persistent tracking cookies. Temporary session states may be retained locally in your browser cache to preserve your sound preferences (such as selected mode) during your active browsing session.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We may use standard, privacy-respecting website analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Google Analytics) solely to analyze aggregate site traffic, device types, and page loading speeds. These analytics do not identify individual users.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally revise this Privacy Policy to reflect technical updates. Any changes will be posted immediately on this page.

5. Contact Information

For any questions or technical support inquiries regarding this tool, you can reach us at contact@yourdomain.com.
